Conference themes

Papers, abstracts and posters are invited under (but not limited to) the 15 themes below. Search by keyword to find the theme that fits your work.

THEME 01

Life Sciences for a Sustainable Healthy Environment

Looks at how discoveries across the biological sciences can protect ecosystems and community health at the same time. The theme connects basic research with practical, low-impact solutions that communities can adopt.

Topics of interest 6
  • Ecosystem services and human wellbeing
  • Bioremediation and green technologies
  • Sustainable agriculture, aquaculture and sericulture
  • Biological indicators for environmental monitoring
  • Biodiversity-based livelihoods
  • Community-based environmental health programmes
SDG 3SDG 13SDG 15
THEME 02

Vector Biology, Ecology and Integrated Management

Covers the biology, behaviour and ecology of disease-carrying insects and other arthropods, and how this knowledge is used to reduce their impact. Contributions on field-ready and community-friendly control strategies are especially welcome.

Topics of interest 6
  • Biology of mosquitoes, sandflies, ticks, flies and mites
  • Breeding habitats, host-seeking and feeding behaviour
  • Insecticide resistance and its mechanisms
  • Integrated vector management (IVM)
  • Biological control, larvicides and botanical insecticides
  • Vector surveillance, mapping and risk stratification
SDG 3SDG 6SDG 11
THEME 03

Emerging and Re-emerging Infectious Diseases

Addresses diseases that are newly appearing or returning to places where they had declined, with a focus on vector-borne infections. Papers on early detection, outbreak response and the drivers of disease spread are invited.

Topics of interest 6
  • Dengue, chikungunya and Zika
  • Malaria and lymphatic filariasis
  • Japanese encephalitis, scrub typhus and leptospirosis
  • Outbreak investigation and rapid response
  • Epidemiological modelling and early warning systems
  • Antimicrobial and antiparasitic resistance
SDG 3SDG 17
THEME 04

One Health: Human, Animal and Environmental Health

Treats the health of people, animals and the environment as one connected system. The theme welcomes work that brings together medicine, veterinary science, ecology and public policy.

Topics of interest 6
  • Zoonotic spillover and reservoir hosts
  • Integrated human–animal–environment surveillance
  • Veterinary public health
  • Water, sanitation and waste-related health risks
  • Cross-sector collaboration and One Health policy
  • Case studies of One Health in practice
SDG 3SDG 6SDG 15
THEME 05

Climate Change, Biodiversity and Ecosystem Health

Examines how a changing climate affects species, habitats and the diseases linked to them. Research on adaptation, restoration and the shifting range of vectors and hosts is encouraged.

Topics of interest 6
  • Climate effects on vector distribution and disease transmission
  • Habitat loss and land-use change
  • Freshwater, coastal and marine ecosystem health
  • Invasive species and biodiversity loss
  • Restoration ecology and nature-based solutions
  • Climate adaptation and community resilience
SDG 13SDG 14SDG 15
THEME 06

Biotechnology and Molecular Innovations in Healthcare

Highlights molecular and biotechnological tools that improve diagnosis, treatment and prevention. The theme includes laboratory advances as well as those moving towards clinical or field use.

Topics of interest 6
  • Recombinant proteins, vaccines and biologics
  • Molecular diagnostics: PCR, LAMP and biosensors
  • Genome editing and gene-based vector control
  • Molecular markers and DNA barcoding
  • Cell culture and tissue engineering
  • Translational and applied biotechnology
SDG 3SDG 9
THEME 07

Microbiology, Immunology and Infectious Disease Research

Focuses on how pathogens cause disease and how the host responds. Studies on infection mechanisms, immunity and the microbes living in and on hosts and vectors fit here.

Topics of interest 6
  • Host–pathogen interactions and pathogenesis
  • Immune responses to vector-borne infections
  • Vaccine immunology and immunotherapy
  • Microbiome of humans, vectors and the environment
  • Clinical and environmental microbiology
  • Diagnostic microbiology and drug resistance
SDG 3
THEME 08

Environmental Pollution, Toxicology and Risk Assessment

Deals with pollutants, their effects on living organisms and how risks are measured and managed. Work on pesticides, metals, plastics and emerging contaminants is invited.

Topics of interest 6
  • Pesticide and insecticide residues
  • Heavy metals and persistent organic pollutants
  • Microplastics and emerging contaminants
  • Ecotoxicology and biomarkers of exposure
  • Water, soil and air quality assessment
  • Health and environmental risk assessment
SDG 3SDG 6SDG 12
THEME 09

Genomics, Bioinformatics and Precision Medicine

Brings together sequence-based science and computational analysis for health and vector research. The theme covers both the generation of genomic data and the methods used to interpret it.

Topics of interest 6
  • Next-generation sequencing and metagenomics
  • Population genomics of vectors and pathogens
  • Comparative and functional genomics
  • Computational biology and databases
  • Machine learning for biological data
  • Pharmacogenomics and personalised medicine
SDG 3SDG 9
THEME 10

Natural Products, Medicinal Plants and Drug Discovery

Explores compounds from plants, microbes and other natural sources for medicine and pest control. Contributions may range from traditional knowledge to laboratory screening and computer-aided design.

Topics of interest 6
  • Phytochemistry and bioactive compounds
  • Plant-based larvicides, repellents and insecticides
  • Antimicrobial, antiparasitic and anticancer screening
  • In silico docking and drug design
  • Ethnopharmacology and traditional knowledge
  • Standardisation and safety of herbal products
SDG 3SDG 15
THEME 11

Nanotechnology and Advanced Therapeutics

Looks at nanoscale materials and new therapeutic approaches in health and vector control. Studies on safety and environmental impact are as welcome as those on performance.

Topics of interest 6
  • Green synthesis of nanoparticles
  • Nano-formulations for vector control
  • Nano-based drug and vaccine delivery
  • Nanosensors and diagnostics
  • Toxicity and environmental safety of nanomaterials
  • Biomaterials and regenerative approaches
SDG 3SDG 9
THEME 12

Artificial Intelligence and Digital Technologies in Life Sciences

Covers the use of data-driven and digital tools in biology and public health. The theme includes disease forecasting, automated identification and technologies that put information in people's hands.

Topics of interest 6
  • Machine learning for disease prediction
  • Remote sensing and GIS for vector mapping
  • Image-based identification of insects and pathogens
  • Mobile apps, drones and citizen science
  • Big data and digital health platforms
  • Ethics and responsible use of AI in health
SDG 3SDG 9SDG 11
THEME 13

Food Safety, Nutrition and Public Health

Addresses how what we eat and drink affects health, from contamination to nutrition. Research on prevention, monitoring and community-level intervention is invited.

Topics of interest 6
  • Foodborne and waterborne pathogens
  • Chemical contaminants and adulteration
  • Nutrition, immunity and disease resistance
  • Functional foods and nutraceuticals
  • Food security and sustainable food systems
  • Hygiene, sanitation and community nutrition
SDG 2SDG 3SDG 6
THEME 14

Wildlife Conservation, Zoonoses and Environmental Sustainability

Connects the protection of wild species with the prevention of diseases shared between animals and people. Papers on field studies, conservation policy and wildlife health are welcome.

Topics of interest 6
  • Wildlife disease and health monitoring
  • Human–wildlife interface and zoonotic risk
  • Conservation of threatened species and habitats
  • Protected-area management
  • Reservoir hosts and disease ecology
  • Sustainable use of natural resources
SDG 14SDG 15
THEME 15

Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and Global Health

Links research and practice to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, especially those on health, water, climate and partnerships. The theme welcomes policy, education and community perspectives alongside science.

Topics of interest 6
  • Health and wellbeing (SDG 3) and universal access
  • Clean water and sanitation (SDG 6)
  • Climate action and life on land and below water (SDGs 13–15)
  • Partnerships for the goals (SDG 17)
  • Health equity and vulnerable communities
  • Capacity building, education and policy translation
SDG 3SDG 6SDG 13SDG 17

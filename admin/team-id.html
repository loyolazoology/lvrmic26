Team ID cards

8.5 cm × 12 cm portrait card with punch-hole circle, logos, photo, QR (scan for name, designation, phone and email), LVRMIC ID and barcode, optional convener sign space, designation and venue. Optional meal punch cards (10 × 14.5 cm) and blank cards for handwriting. Choose who to print, generate, then print or download the PDF.

Tap a day or a meal name to tick its whole row or column. Only the ticked meals and days appear on each card, one punch circle per day.

In the print window set margins to None and turn on Background graphics. Or press Download PDF for a ready file. Punch cards print on A4 sheets, 4 per page (10 × 14.5 cm each), with the punch circles on the left and right edges for a standard 6 mm hole punch.

Blank cards for handwriting

Name, ID and photo are left empty to fill by hand. Four cards fit on one A4 sheet. For blank meal punch cards, tick the meals and days in the list above. The sign slot follows the category setting in Card settings.

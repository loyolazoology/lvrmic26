Meal coupons

Tick days and meals first (tap a day or meal name to tick its row or column). Only ticked meals print, one punch circle per day. Each coupon shows name, LVRMIC ID and VEG / NON-VEG from the sheet. A4 sheet, 4 coupons per page (10 × 14.5 cm).

Blank badges and coupons for handwriting

No photo or QR. Blank badges have LVRMIC26- with four ID boxes (the faint 1 is the spot-registration hint, write over it if needed), lines for name, designation and institution, a Remarks space and the convener sign. Blank coupons use the ticked meals above and have Veg / Non-Veg tick boxes. The theme and sign settings above apply.

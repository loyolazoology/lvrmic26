  About the conference

Where entomology, environment & public health meet

Vector-borne diseases remain one of the most pressing public health challenges in tropical regions. This conference brings together entomologists, molecular biologists, environmental scientists, and policy makers to share research on vector biology, disease surveillance, and sustainable environmental management — building practical pathways from laboratory research to community-level risk reduction.

Read more about the conference

Conference objectives

Foster interdisciplinary dialogue on vector-borne disease risk, share emerging molecular and ecological research, and translate science into sustainable, community-ready environmental health strategies.

  Beyond the conference

Places to visit around Chennai

Delegates travelling from outside Chennai may like to set aside time to explore the city and its surroundings during their visit:

Marina Beach

One of the longest urban beaches in the world, a short drive from the campus.

Kapaleeshwarar Temple

A landmark Dravidian-style temple in Mylapore, rich in history and architecture.

San Thome Basilica

A historic basilica on the Bay of Bengal, built over the tomb of St. Thomas the Apostle.

Government Museum, Egmore

One of the oldest museums in India, with archaeology, numismatics and natural history galleries.

DakshinaChitra Heritage Museum

A living-history museum celebrating South Indian art, architecture and craft.

Mahabalipuram (day trip)

UNESCO World Heritage shore temples and rock-cut monuments, about 1.5–2 hours from the city.

T. Nagar & Pondy Bazaar

Popular shopping districts for textiles, souvenirs and local street food.

Guindy National Park

A protected green space within the city, home to blackbuck and a snake park.

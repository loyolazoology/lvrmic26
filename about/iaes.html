  Who we are

About the Academy

The Indian Academy of Environmental Science (IAES) has introduced a number of medals, certificates and honorary fellowships for active researchers, academicians and environmental activists, related to many aspects of eco-biological science.

Environmental Science

A focus on eco-biological and environmental disciplines, and on the people who advance them.

Encouraging Young Scientists

Recognition that encourages emerging researchers to make a mark early in their careers.

Honouring Senior Researchers

Due recognition for senior researchers with long careers in the related disciplines.

Environmental Activism

Awards are open to environmental activists as well as researchers and academicians.

  How to apply

Applying for an IAES award

  1. 1

    Get the application form

    Download the pro-forma from the IAES Award page (PDF or Word), or request it from the Secretary or President.

  2. 2

    Fill it in with your full biodata

    The application must be submitted along with the full biodata of the applicant, online.

  3. 3

    Submit with the demand draft

    Mail the duly filled form and demand draft by registered post, or send them by e-mail, to the Secretary or President.

Eligibility and experience requirements differ by award — check each category on the IAES Award page before applying.

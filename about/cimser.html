About CIMSER

Where Insect Science Meets the Environment

The Centre for Insect Molecular Studies and Environmental Research (CIMSER) was established on 19 December 2018 with the blessings of Rev. Dr. F. Andrew, S.J.

Dr. B. Vasantharaj David, Chairman of the Scientific/Academic Board, International Institute of Biotechnology and Toxicology, unveiled the CIMSER logo. The occasion was graced by Dr. M. G. Ragunathan, Assistant Professor, Gurunanak College, Chennai, and Dr. J. Mary Vimala Kalaiarasi, Head of the Department, PG and Research Department of Advanced Zoology and Biotechnology, Loyola College.

Under the leadership of its Director, Dr. M. Raja, CIMSER has successfully conducted many State, National and International level seminars and conferences.

Research Supervisor & Conference Convener

Dr. M. Raja is an Assistant Professor at Loyola College and the Director of CIMSER. With around 14 years of academic experience, his expertise spans entomology, biocontrol and insect molecular biology. He is a Research Supervisor at CIMSER and has guided and supervised many students and research scholars — four scholars have so far completed their Ph.D. degrees under his guideship.

He has successfully conducted many State, National and International level seminars and conferences, and serves as the Convener of the Conference “Emerging Challenges in the Prevention and Control of Vector-Borne Diseases”.

New Initiative by CIMSER

M-BEAT: Student-Led Mosquito Control

Loyola College · CIMSER

M-BEAT

Stop mosquitoes where they start.

Mosquito Breeding Site Elimination and Assessment Team — student-led teams that find and remove breeding sources on Chennai college campuses.

Register as a student

What M-BEAT sets out to do

An initiative by CIMSER, PG & Research Department of Advanced Zoology and Biotechnology, Loyola College (Autonomous), Chennai, with Greater Chennai Corporation and AHC Foundation.

  • Identify and eliminate mosquito breeding sources within participating college premises through regular surveillance and source-reduction activities by student-led teams.
  • Establish Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for maintaining mosquito-breeding-free campuses.
  • Promote Behaviour Change Communication (BCC) activities to update college authorities, faculty, students and support staff on vector surveillance, source reduction and preventive measures.
  • Institutionalize periodic inspections, self-monitoring and reporting mechanisms in collaboration with Corporation officials.
  • Recognize and certify colleges that consistently maintain breeding-free premises based on predefined criteria.
  • Develop participating colleges as model institutions that can help replicate this initiative across other educational institutions in Chennai.

What students gain

Practical exposure that encourages you to take an active role in environmental health, vector surveillance and community service.

  • M-BEAT Membership Certificate
  • Environmental Volunteer Recognition
  • Mosquito & Vector-Control Field Experience
  • Practical Field Assessment Experience
  • Greater Chennai Corporation Collaboration Experience
  • CV / Resume Enhancement
  • Community Service Experience

  About CIMSER

Where insect science meets the environment

The Centre for Insect Molecular Studies and Environmental Research (CIMSER) was established on 19 December 2018 with the blessings of Rev. Dr. F. Andrew, S.J. Under the leadership of its Director, Dr. M. Raja, CIMSER has successfully conducted many State, National and International level seminars and conferences.

Insect Molecular Biology

Research on insect biology at the molecular level, supporting biocontrol and sustainable pest management.

Biocontrol & Biopesticides

Eco-friendly alternatives that reduce dependence on chemical pesticides in agriculture and public health.

Vector-Borne Diseases

Work towards the prevention and control of vector-borne diseases through research, outreach and community action.

Seminars & Conferences

A regular programme of State, National and International seminars and conferences for researchers and students.

Research supervisor & conference convener

Dr. M. Raja is an Assistant Professor at Loyola College and the Director of CIMSER. With around 14 years of academic experience, his expertise spans entomology, biocontrol and insect molecular biology. He has guided many students and research scholars — four scholars have so far completed their Ph.D. degrees under his guidance.

He has successfully conducted many State, National and International level seminars and conferences, and serves as the Convener of VRMIC 2026. He is a Fellow of the Entomological Society of India (F.E.S.I.) and of the Royal Entomological Society, London (F.R.E.S).

  New initiative by CIMSER

M-BEAT: student-led mosquito control

M-BEAT

Stop mosquitoes where they start.

Mosquito Breeding Site Elimination and Assessment Team — student-led teams that find and remove breeding sources on Chennai college campuses.

Register as a student

What M-BEAT sets out to do

An initiative by CIMSER, Loyola College (Autonomous), with Greater Chennai Corporation and AHC Foundation.

  • Identify and eliminate mosquito breeding sources within participating college premises through regular surveillance and source reduction by student-led teams.
  • Establish Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for mosquito-breeding-free campuses.
  • Promote Behaviour Change Communication (BCC) on vector surveillance, source reduction and preventive measures.
  • Institutionalize periodic inspections, self-monitoring and reporting with Corporation officials.
  • Recognize and certify colleges that consistently maintain breeding-free premises.
  • Develop model institutions to replicate the initiative across Chennai.

What students gain

Practical exposure to environmental health, vector surveillance and community service.

  • M-BEAT Membership Certificate
  • Environmental Volunteer Recognition
  • Mosquito & Vector-Control Field Experience
  • Practical Field Assessment Experience
  • Greater Chennai Corporation Collaboration Experience
  • CV / Resume Enhancement
  • Community Service Experience
